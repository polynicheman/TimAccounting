The Scientist

Six principles, deconstructed to first principles

Accounting isn't a set of rules to memorise — it's a system of physics. We reduced it to six irreducible laws and built every calculation, every validation and every screen on top of them.

01

Duality

Value is never created or destroyed inside the books, only moved. Every entry must leave the equation in balance — no exceptions, ever.

Assets = Liabilities + Equity
02

Periodicity

A business's life is continuous, but measurement needs boundaries. Time is a first-class entity, enabling flawless comparison across months, quarters and years.

Period → Report → Compare
03

Objectivity

Every entry must be backed by verifiable evidence. Apex digitises this as Source Documents — any figure traces back to the paper behind it.

Entry → Document → Proof
04

Matching

Revenue is recognised in the same period as the expenses that produced it. Apex automates these cause-and-effect links wherever it can.

Revenue ⇄ Expense (same period)
05

Conservatism

When in doubt, never overstate assets or income. A cautionary logic layer flags aggressive treatments before they reach your reports.

When uncertain → understate
06

Materiality

Significance is judged by impact on a decision. A configurable threshold filters out trivial noise so the signal stays visible.

Impact > Threshold → Disclose
